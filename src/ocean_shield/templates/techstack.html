ENGINE 01

Multi-Modal Satellite & Met-Ocean Ingestion

Sentinel-1 C-Band SAR Sentinel-2 Optical (L2A) HYCOM GOFS 3.1 NetCDF NOAA GFS Winds MarineCadastre AIS

Streams multi-sensor feeds to guarantee 24/7 all-weather maritime domain awareness. SAR penetrates cloud cover and darkness; optical data calculates spectral indices; NetCDF grids provide 4D velocity vectors.

  • SAR Resolution: 10m spatial resolution, C-Band (5.405 GHz), VV/VH polarizations.
  • Hydrodynamics: 4D NetCDF-4 grids ($u$ zonal, $v$ meridional current velocities in m/s).
  • AIS Kinematics: Real-time and archival Class-A transponder broadcast records (MMSI, SOG, COG, Heading).

ENGINE 02

Deep Learning Neural Perception & Super-Resolution

PyTorch 2.0+ U-Net Architecture Dice-BCE Loss ESPCN 2X Super-Res Adaptive CA-CFAR

Identifies low-backscatter dark patches where mineral oil dampens capillary surface waves. Employs 2D sliding Hann-window overlapping tiles ($256\times 256$) to segment full satellite scenes in $<3.0$ seconds without out-of-memory errors.

  • Segmentation Model: U-Net with Skip Connections preserving thin tail boundaries.
  • Super-Resolution: Efficient Sub-Pixel Convolutional Network (ESPCN) sharpens 10m radar pixels.
  • Metallic Ship Detection: CA-CFAR radar thresholding extracts point contacts ($>25\text{ dB}$).
  • Bonn Agreement Standards: Calculates slick area ($0.62\text{ km}^2$) and mass ($14\text{ tonnes}$).

ENGINE 03

4th-Order Runge-Kutta Hydrodynamic Drift & Weathering

Runge-Kutta 4 (RK4) Lagrangian Swarm Mackay ADIOS Weathering NumPy Vectorization Coriolis Deflection

Oil moves with winds and currents. This engine operates in dual directions: Hindcasting (Rewinding) to find the spill origin coordinates, and Forecasting to issue coastal reef landfall warnings.

  • Numerical Accuracy: RK4 truncation error $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^4)$ avoids Euler trajectory spiral errors.
  • Wind Drift Factor: $3.2\%$ of 10m wind velocity with $12^\circ$ rightward Coriolis deflection.
  • Swarm Resolution: 1,000 Lagrangian particles advected across 4D velocity fields.
  • Chemical Weathering: Simulates evaporation (28%), emulsification mousse (62%), and viscosity surge (480 cP).
# RK4 Trajectory Integration Equation k1 = f(t_n, y_n) k2 = f(t_n + dt/2, y_n + dt*k1/2) k3 = f(t_n + dt/2, y_n + dt*k2/2) k4 = f(t_n + dt, y_n + dt*k3) y_next = y_n + (dt/6) * (k1 + 2*k2 + 2*k3 + k4)
ENGINE 04

Forensic AIS Kinematics & Dark Vessel Attribution

Haversine Spatial Indexing Kinematic Speed Anomaly Dark Vessel Detection MARPOL Annex I Scoring

Bridges physical hydrodynamics with maritime law enforcement. Correlates vessel historical trajectories against the calculated spill epicenter $(x_0, y_0, t_0)$ and flags speed anomalies characteristic of illegal bilge dumping.

  • Closest Point of Approach (CPA): Measures minimum spatial distance in nautical miles at $t_0$.
  • Speed Drop Detection: Flags commercial vessels decelerating from 14 knots to 3–4 knots during transit.
  • Radar-to-AIS Cross-Check: Flags metallic radar targets lacking transponder broadcasts as Non-Cooperative Dark Vessels.
  • Weighted Suspect Score: Combines spatial proximity ($40\%$), temporal alignment ($25\%$), speed anomaly ($25\%$), and ship class prior ($10\%$).

⚖️ Architectural Decision Matrix (Why We Built It This Way)

JUDGE DEFENSE JUSTIFICATIONS

Why PyTorch U-Net instead of YOLO?

YOLO outputs rigid bounding boxes. Fluid oil spills have amorphous, non-convex fractal edges that shear with currents. Bounding boxes overestimate slick area by up to 300%. U-Net provides exact pixel-level semantic segmentation necessary for calculating Bonn Agreement mass estimates.

Why Runge-Kutta 4 instead of Euler?

Euler integration assumes constant velocity across time, accumulating $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$ error that spirals particles away in reversing tidal channels. 4th-Order Runge-Kutta (RK4) samples 4 velocity vectors per step, achieving $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^4)$ truncation accuracy across complex coral reefs.

Why NetCDF-4 instead of GeoJSON for Currents?

NetCDF-4 is the global scientific standard for 4D multidimensional grids. Storing 3D ocean velocity fields ($time, lat, lon, u, v$) in JSON would produce 50 MB files with slow parsing. NetCDF stores binary arrays with sub-millisecond indexed slicing.

Why FastAPI instead of Flask / Django?

FastAPI runs on asynchronous ASGI (Starlette + Uvicorn) capable of handling concurrent satellite tile processing with automatic Pydantic type validation, sub-millisecond route dispatching, and built-in interactive OpenAPI/Swagger documentation.
