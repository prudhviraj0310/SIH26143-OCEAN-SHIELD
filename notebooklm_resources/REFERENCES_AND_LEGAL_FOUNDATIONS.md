## 1. Statutory & Maritime Law Foundations (Legal Admissibility)

Evidence generated by Ocean Shield is structured to meet evidentiary admissibility standards under the **Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (Section 65B: Electronic Records)** and the following international treaties:

1. **MARPOL 73/78 Annex I (Regulations for the Prevention of Pollution by Oil):**
   * *Regulation 15 & 34:* Discharges outside special areas are strictly prohibited unless vessel is proceeding en route, instantaneous discharge rate does not exceed 30 liters per nautical mile, and oil content of effluent without dilution does not exceed 15 ppm.
   * *Legal Nexus:* Our 5-factor Bayesian matrix establishes the kinematic "discharge window" (<3 knots, route deviation, darkness/nighttime pumping).

2. **United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS, 1982):**
   * *Article 194:* Measures to prevent, reduce and control pollution of the marine environment.
   * *Article 211:* Laws and regulations concerning maritime pollution from vessels.
   * *Article 217 & 220:* Enforcement by coastal States in the Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) up to 200 nautical miles.

3. **Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 (Act No. 44 of 1958):**
   * *Part XIA (Sections 356A–356O):* Prevention and containment of pollution of the sea by oil; empowering the Director General of Shipping and the Indian Coast Guard to detain offending vessels.

4. **IMO Resolution A.917(22) (Guidelines for Shipborne AIS):**
   * Mandates continuous VHF broadcast of maritime mobile service identity (MMSI), coordinates, SOG, COG, heading, and navigational status.
   * *Spoofing & Disabling Defense:* Ocean Shield detects intentional AIS transponder blackout zones when transiting high-risk EEZ corridors.

## 2. Remote Sensing & Radar Signal Processing (SAR & Optical)

1. **Lee, J. S. (1980):** *"Digital image enhancement and noise filtering by use of local statistics."* IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, 2(2), 165–168.
   * *Implementation:* Enhanced Lee speckle suppression in `sar_engine.py` using local variance-to-mean ratios to preserve slick edge gradients.
2. **Skolnik, M. (2008):** *Radar Handbook (3rd ed.).* McGraw-Hill.
   * *Implementation:* Cell-Averaging Constant False Alarm Rate (CA-CFAR) filter for dual-polarization (VV/VH) Sentinel-1 backscatter damping.
3. **Solberg, A. H. S., Brekke, C., & Husøy, P. O. (2007):** *"Oil spill detection in SAR imagery: algorithms and performance."* IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 45(3), 746–755.
   * *Implementation:* Feature extraction: area, perimeter, elongation, spread, and Weber contrast.
4. **Höhle, J., & Höhle, M. (2009):** *"Accuracy assessment of digital elevation models by means of robust statistical methods."* ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, 64(4), 398–406.
   * *Implementation:* Normalized Median Absolute Deviation (NMAD), LE90, and LE95 in `elevation_engine.py` for outlier-immune geodetic DSM evaluation.

## 3. Physical Hydrodynamics & Slick Weathering Kinetics

1. **Runge, C. (1895) & Kutta, M. W. (1901):** *"Numerical solution of systems of differential equations."*
   * *Implementation:* 4th-Order Runge-Kutta (RK4) numerical integrator in `drift_engine.py`:
     $$\vec{x}_{t+\Delta t} = \vec{x}_t + \frac{\Delta t}{6}(k_1 + 2k_2 + 2k_3 + k_4)$$
     preventing secular truncation errors present in 1st-order Euler models.
2. **Fay, J. A. (1971):** *"Physical processes in the spread of oil on a water surface."* Proceedings of the Joint Conference on Prevention and Control of Oil Spills, API, 463–467.
   * *Implementation:* Three distinct radial spreading regimes: Gravity-Inertia, Gravity-Viscous, and Surface Tension-Viscous.
3. **Mackay, D., Buist, I., Mascarenhas, R., & Paterson, S. (1980):** *"Oil spill processes and models."* Environment Canada Report EPS 3-EC-80-1.
   * *Implementation:* Analytical evaporative exposure fraction and mousse water-in-oil emulsification kinetics.
4. **Coriolis, G.-G. (1835):** *"Sur les équations du mouvement relatif des systèmes de corps."*
   * *Implementation:* 15° clockwise Ekman deflection angle applied to 3% surface wind shear in the Northern Hemisphere.

## 4. Spatio-Temporal Forensics & Metric Verification

1. **Dubuisson, M. P., & Jain, A. K. (1994):** *"A Modified Hausdorff Distance for object matching."* Proceedings of 12th International Conference on Pattern Recognition (ICPR), IEEE, Vol. 1, 566–568.
   * *Implementation:* Counterfactual geometric validation metric in `drift_engine.py`:
     $$d_{\text{MHD}}(S, O) = \max\left( \frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{s \in S} \min_{o \in O} d(s, o), \frac{1}{|O|}\sum_{o \in O} \min_{s \in S} d(s, o) \right)$$
2. **Huber, P. J. (1964):** *"Robust estimation of a location parameter."* The Annals of Mathematical Statistics, 35(1), 73–101.
   * *Implementation:* Iteratively Reweighted Least Squares (IRLS) Huber scale calibration in `elevation_engine.py` ($\delta = 1.5$) ensuring scale fitting resists vertical outliers.
